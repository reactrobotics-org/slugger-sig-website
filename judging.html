Judging & awards

Engineering notebook deadline
11:59 PM ET · November 21, 2026

Notebooks are submitted digitally as a link uploaded in the “My Account” panel of the team contact. Reviews are conducted remotely before the event, so make sure your link is live and accessible no later than two weeks out.

Judging format

Remote initial interviews, with follow-up interviews across the competition days as needed. Judges come from the local business and education community, including engineering professors from the University of Louisville and the University of Kentucky.

Impartiality

No parents, coaches, or anyone closely affiliated with a participating organization may judge their own team at this event. Interested in judging? Email volunteer@reactrobotics.org.
