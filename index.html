Louisville Slugger Signature Event

VEX Robotics returns to Louisville, Kentucky, home to years of unforgettable VEX World Championship events. This first-of-its-kind V5RC tournament is hosted by REACT Robotics in partnership with Kentucky’s most experienced tournament organizations.

Event staff

Our Head Referee is the legendary Spencer Yates. The Judge Advisor is being finalized; expect another highly experienced VEX veteran to join the event staff.

Custom awards

Trophies are custom-crafted by the Louisville Slugger Museum from the same wood used for Louisville Slugger bats. They are exclusive to this event, and a keepsake your team will treasure.

Holiday downtown

The event lands in the holiday season, with the whole downtown decorated for Christmas. Leave time for the lights, the decorations and the festive atmosphere.

Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory

Discounted factory tours for every event attendee.

See how every Louisville Slugger wood bat used by Major League Baseball players is made, from a 4×4 billet of wood to one of the most iconic pieces of sports equipment in the world.
